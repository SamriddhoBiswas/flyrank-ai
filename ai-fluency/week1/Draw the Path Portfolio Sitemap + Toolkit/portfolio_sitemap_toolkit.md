# W1: FL-02 — Draw the Path: Portfolio Sitemap + Toolkit

## Initial Sitemap

I designed the portfolio as a single-page site with four sections:

1. **Hero / Claim** — Quickly communicate my main claim, background, SDE/GenAI focus, and direct the visitor toward my work.
2. **Proof / Evidence** — Demonstrate the claim through DSA/LeetCode practice, full-stack projects, GenAI projects, and specific technical implementation details.
3. **About / Context** — Provide brief information about my B.Tech CSE background, interests, and career direction.
4. **Contact / CTA** — Give the visitor a clear way to contact me about an SDE/GenAI opportunity, along with LinkedIn and GitHub.

The intended visitor journey was:

**Claim → Proof → Context → Action**

## Claude Project

I created a Claude Project named **“Portfolio — General AI Fluency** and configured it with my proof statement and custom instructions asking Claude to act as a portfolio-building tutor and critical thinking partner.

The instructions tell Claude to challenge vague claims, question unnecessary portfolio elements, use only evidence from my real experience, and help me make the portfolio focused and evidence-driven rather than simply writing it for me.

## Pressure Test

I asked Claude to critically evaluate the sitemap against three things:

- Whether it strengthens my single claim.
- Whether it helps a software engineering hiring manager or technical recruiter believe that claim.
- Whether it moves that person toward contacting me.

Claude identified two important weaknesses:

1. The separate **About / Context** section did not directly prove my claim or move the visitor toward the desired action, so it was better to fold that information into the Hero and Contact sections.
2. **DSA/LeetCode and my projects appeared as two separate skill categories** rather than clearly demonstrating how algorithmic thinking is applied inside real software.

Claude also recommended making the resume easily accessible and keeping a direct Contact path available from the Hero.

## Change Made After Pressure Testing

I revised the sitemap from four sections to three:

**Hero / Claim → Proof / Evidence → Contact / CTA**

The separate About section was removed, with relevant background information such as my B.Tech CSE and SDE/GenAI focus moved into the Hero.

I also changed the Proof section so that the connection between my DSA practice and software projects is explicit:

**Algorithmic Thinking → Applied in Projects → Real Software Built → Impact / Outcome**

This makes the portfolio's central proof more focused and ensures that DSA is not presented as an unrelated skill beside my projects.

The revised portfolio is therefore designed to move a hiring manager or recruiter through:

**Understanding the claim → Seeing the proof → Taking action**
