## Three Target Tasks & Success Definitions

### 1. Writing Backend Code for FlyRank Internship

**Task:** Use AI selectively while developing backend functionality, without outsourcing the core implementation work.

**Done well means:**
- Code passes all required test cases/endpoints on first review submission.
- No more than 1 round of AI-assisted debugging is needed before it is submission-ready.
- I understand and can explain the implementation myself.

### 2. Allied Shipping Agency Website Work

**Task:** Use AI to assist with website structure, content, design decisions, and implementation while maintaining consistency with the project brief.

**Done well means:**
- Each section matches the content brief and design system (navy/gold/beige).
- No more than 2 revision rounds are needed before the work is considered final.
- The final implementation remains consistent and understandable to me.

### 3. Drafting Professional Content

**Task:** Use AI to draft and pressure-test resume content, outreach messages, and LinkedIn bios.

**Done well means:**
- Every draft goes through AI pressure-testing before sending.
- No claim is included that I cannot defend under a direct follow-up question.
- The final wording remains accurate to my actual experience and responsibilities.
